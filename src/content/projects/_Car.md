In Feburary 2025, I purchased a 2003 'Cherry Black' Mazda MX-5 NB2 for about £3500. It had 34k miles on the dash and lived in a garage. Since then, it's sat in the perpetual Manchester rain and needs some significant maintenance. I've got further plans for minor cosmetic tweaks but aim to preserve the original stock look of the car.

## External work.

The paintwork needs a deep clean, wax and polish. It's colour, named 'Cherry Black' shined a deep purple-crimson colour which I'd like to bring back out.

The driver-side indicator bracket is damaged so over speedbumps, the indicator can fall out of the car, dangling from it's cables.

There is noticible rust damage in the rear wheel arches. I have been recommended a specialist in my local area and while the cost might be significant, I don't think I can handle losing this car to rust. I can see why Lotus' cars are made from fiberglass.

I also wish to purchase a hard top for the car, ideally heated. It should probably be obtained before I have the paintwork treated, so the colour can be matched to the car's own.
