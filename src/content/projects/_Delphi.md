In 2021, Spotify launched the 'Car Thing'. It was a music streaming device that allowed for voice and touch controls over the car's music system via Spotify. It was bricked by Spotify in 2024 as a result of poor sales. Since then, alternative custom firmware has been developed by the community such as Nocturne and DeskThing.

This project consists of two devices. A Spotify Desk Thing device will be used as a desk utility device for macros and music control whilst a Playstation PSP will be repurposed for my car's navigation and music control system.

'Delphi' was the name of one of Apollo's oracles. This project uses that name as it provides information as well as supports music.

This project requires a Spotify Car Thing device which can be obtained from American ebay suppliers for about £90.
