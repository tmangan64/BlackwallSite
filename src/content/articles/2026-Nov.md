# Where I've been

Recently I've started a position as a Cyber Analyst at a CSIRT where I've been introduced to all sorts of technologies and ideas. I've just made a purchase for 2x hardware keys from a Swiss company called 'Token2'; the keys themselves are cheaper than Yubikeys, have FIDO level 2 certification (unlike Yubikey's level 1) and hold 300 resident keys which is something I quite like. My goal is to mostly eliminate passwords in my personal security systems by introducing FIDO2 hardware-key authorisation and given my password vault currently holds over 500 credentials, as many resident keys as possible is ideal.


I've also been designing my own keyboard PCB from scratch for a Lily58-styled hall effect keyboard, something the industry doesn't really offer. I've been following KiCAD tutorials and have nearly finished building the left half of the keyboard's first iteration. I'd later like to integrate a trackpad into the right half, giving me the ability to either use a mouse with WASD while gaming or to save on deskspace and avoid using a mouse entirely while working.
