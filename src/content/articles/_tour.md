# The history of the Grand Tour

The tradition of travelling at the dawn of adulthood dates back many years and was originally taken around Europe. Historian Edward Gibbon once said "According to the law of custom, and perhaps of reason, foreign travel completes the education of an English gentleman." The purpose of the tour is to surround yourself with languages, people and places you've never encountered before to return home a changed and better-educated person.

# Plan

The goal is to travel around South-East Asia, starting in Vietnam.
