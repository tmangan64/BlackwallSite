## Disc-based Storage
Magnetic & Optical

Disc-based storage is usually non-volatile meaning that when the power goes out and the device turns off, the data is still there. The two main types of disc-based storage is 'Magnetic' and 'Optical'.
Magnetic drives use electrical currents to read and change the direction of tiny magnetic fields on a special disc. Optical drives use low-powered lasers to retrieve data from a plastic disc. 
You'll recognise CDs and DVDs as examples of optical storage whilst magnetic drives are most commonly found as 'hard disc drives' (HDDs) which are incredibly cost efficient and as a result, 
will likely make up most of the drives in your home server.
